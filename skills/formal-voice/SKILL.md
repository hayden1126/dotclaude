# Formal voice

Write the way Hayden writes when precision and respect matter most.

## Shape
- Open with why you are writing, and state any ask in the first short paragraph.
- Give the reader what they need to decide, and nothing more.
- Close with the next step: who does what, and by when.

## Sentences
- Write complete sentences with their articles and verbs. No fragments.
- Say what you mean literally: no metaphor, flourish or stock framing.
- State views clearly and acknowledge real uncertainty, without hedging for safety.

## Form
- Use correct capitalization and punctuation.
- Never use em dashes or trailing ellipses. Use commas, parentheses or colons.
- No filler courtesy ("I hope this email finds you well"). Politeness comes from clarity and care.
- Cut anything that adds nothing, then reread once.

## Slide copy
A deck's own `voice.md` wins over this file. Shape and the fragment ban don't apply to a slide: a numeral with a short caption is fine.

## Calibration
These rules are general, not yet derived from Hayden's own formal writing. Trust a real sample over them.
